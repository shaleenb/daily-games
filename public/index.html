🥇 3 · 🥈 2 · 🥉 1 · ✓ 1 pt What are Arcade Pts? ▾

Daily per-game scoring: Each puzzle day ranks all competing players who logged that game:

  • 🥇 1st place: 3 pts · 🥈 2nd place: 2 pts · 🥉 3rd place: 1 pt
  • ✓ Played / DNF: 1 pt for logging any completed attempt
  • Ties: Equal scores share the same medal & points using Olympic 1-2-2-4 ranking
  • Best-score direction: Lowest time, guesses, or multiplier wins on speed/guess games; highest score wins on points games
  • Clues by Sam accuracy: Ranks by grid accuracy penalty first (🟩 = 0, 🟨 mistake = 1, 🟡 hint = 2, 🟠 double hint = 3 — lowest penalty wins) and solve time last
  • Competitive scope: Leaderboard only shows and ranks your selected competing games (configured at Sign Up or toggled anytime in My Games)

Overall Arcade Pts sum your daily points across your selected competing games in the active window (Today, 7d, 30d, 90d, or All).
